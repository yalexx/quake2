Qwasm2

An unofficial port of the Quake 2 engine for your browser!

This page will:

  • Load the bundled pak0.pak demo data.
  • Play the demo version (first three levels) of Quake 2.
  • Run the full game, if you supply PAK0.PAK, and optionally PAK1.PAK + PAK2.PAK.

You can also run Qwasm (based on Quake), Dwasm (based on DOOM), or Wwasm (based on RTCW).

 

 |  Gamepads and local saving are supported.
